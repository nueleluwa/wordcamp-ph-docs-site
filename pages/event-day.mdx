# Event Day

The runbook and issue-response playbook for running the event itself.

### [Event Day Operations Runbook](event-day/operations-runbook)
Step-by-step instructions for running event day. Share this document with every organizer and team lead — everyone on-site should know what happens when, and who is where. For what to do when something goes wrong, see issue-response-playbook.

### [Issue Response Playbook](event-day/issue-response-playbook)
A pre-agreed response and owner for the problems most likely to happen on event day, so nobody is improvising a decision-making process in the moment. This is the event-day extension of accountability-and-escalation.
