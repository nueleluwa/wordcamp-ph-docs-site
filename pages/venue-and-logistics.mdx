# Venue & Logistics

Power, internet, venue, catering, and vendor management for the Nigerian context.

### [Nigerian Logistics Checklist](venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist)
Addresses the specific logistical realities of running an event in Nigeria — this is not a generic event checklist with local color added; power, internet, and transportation are planned for as first-class requirements from the start, per welcome-and-philosophy.

### [Vendor & Contracts Tracker](venue-and-logistics/vendor-and-contracts-tracker)
Every external vendor commitment WCPH depends on, tracked as a signed contract — not a verbal agreement or a WhatsApp message.
