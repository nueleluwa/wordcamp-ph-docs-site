# Welcome & Philosophy

**Purpose:** Give anyone joining the team — new organizer or new volunteer — the mental model behind every decision WCPH makes, before they touch a task.

## What WordCamp Port Harcourt is

WordCamp Port Harcourt (WCPH) is a community-organized event bringing together WordPress enthusiasts, developers, designers, bloggers, and digital professionals from Port Harcourt and Nigeria's South-South region.

| | |
|---|---|
| Event name | WordCamp Port Harcourt |
| Location | Port Harcourt, Nigeria |
| Expected attendance | 150–400 attendees |
| Target audience | WordPress developers, designers, bloggers, freelancers, digital agencies, students |
| Governing body | WordCamp Central / WordPress Foundation |

## The four principles that override everything else

### 1. Community first
All actions must support the growth of the WordPress community. When a decision is ambiguous, resolve it in favor of community benefit over individual or organizational gain — including the organizing team's own convenience.

### 2. Volunteer-driven
Organizers and volunteers are **not employees**. Every plan should minimize burnout and distribute responsibility evenly. If one person is carrying a disproportionate share of the work, that is a planning failure to fix, not a sacrifice to praise. See [organisation/accountability-and-escalation.md](../organisation/accountability-and-escalation).

### 3. Built for the Nigerian context
Plans are not "international best practice with a local venue" — they are designed around local realities from day one:
- Power reliability and backup generators
- Internet stability and backup connectivity
- Traffic logistics and transportation coordination
- Sponsor payment methods (bank transfer is the default, not card)

This shows up concretely in [venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist.md](../venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist).

### 4. Accountability culture
Every task in this operating system must have:
- A named, responsible person (not a team — a person)
- A clear deadline
- A progress-check mechanism (a dashboard, a weekly report, a standing meeting item)

If you find a task without one of these three, that's a documentation gap — flag it, don't just work around it. Log it in [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](../organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps).

## WordCamp Central guidelines — the non-negotiables

WCPH operates inside rules set by WordCamp Central and the WordPress Foundation. The full handbook is at https://make.wordpress.org/community/handbook/wordcamp-organizer/. The five rules every organizer must internalize:

1. All WordCamps must be **not-for-profit** events.
2. Events must be **open to all participants**, regardless of experience level.
3. **Speaker slots cannot be purchased or influenced by sponsorship.** A Platinum sponsor gets visibility, not a guaranteed talk.
4. **All organizing team members are volunteers.** No paid organizer roles.
5. **Financial transparency is required.** See [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance) and [planning/budget-and-finance-principles.md](../planning/budget-and-finance-principles).
