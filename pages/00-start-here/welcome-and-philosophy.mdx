# Welcome & Philosophy

**Purpose:** Give anyone joining the team, new organizer or new volunteer, the mental model behind every decision WCPHC makes, before they touch a task.

## What WordCamp Port Harcourt is

WordCamp Port Harcourt (WCPHC) is a community-organized event bringing together WordPress enthusiasts, developers, designers, bloggers, and digital professionals from Port Harcourt and Nigeria's South-South region.

## The four principles that override everything else

### 1. Community first
All actions must support the growth of the WordPress community. When a decision is unclear, resolve it in favor of community benefit over individual or organizational gain. That includes the organizing team's own convenience.

### 2. Volunteer-driven
Organizers and volunteers are **not employees**. Every plan should minimize burnout and spread responsibility evenly. If one person is carrying more than their share of the work, that's a planning failure to fix, not a sacrifice to praise. See [organisation/accountability-and-escalation.md](../organisation/accountability-and-escalation).

### 3. Built for the Nigerian context
Plans aren't "international best practice with a local venue." They're designed around local realities from day one:
- Power reliability and backup generators
- Internet stability and backup connectivity
- Traffic logistics and transportation coordination
- Sponsor payment methods (bank transfer is the default, not card)

This shows up concretely in [venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist.md](../venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist).

### 4. Accountability culture
Every task in this operating system needs:
- A named, responsible person (a person, not a team)
- A clear deadline
- A progress-check mechanism (a dashboard, a weekly report, a standing meeting item)

If you find a task missing any of these three things, that's a documentation gap. Flag it, don't just work around it. Log it in [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](../organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps).

## The non-negotiable WordCamp Central guidelines

WCPHC operates inside rules set by WordCamp Central and the WordPress Foundation. The full handbook is at https://make.wordpress.org/community/handbook/wordcamp-organizer/. Five rules every organizer must know:

1. All WordCamps must be **not-for-profit** events.
2. Events must be **open to all participants**, regardless of experience level.
3. **Speaker slots cannot be purchased or influenced by sponsorship.** A Platinum sponsor gets visibility, not a guaranteed talk.
4. **All organizing team members are volunteers.** No paid organizer roles.
5. **Financial transparency is required.** See [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance) and [planning/budget-and-finance-principles.md](../planning/budget-and-finance-principles).
