# Post-Event

Everything from the 48-hour thank-yous through the 1-month financial closeout.

### [Post-Event Workflow](post-event/post-event-workflow)
The post-event period is critical for maintaining community momentum and improving future events. This workflow ensures nothing is missed between the last attendee leaving and the team formally closing out the event.

### [Post-Event Report Guide](post-event/event-report-guide)
The structure for the comprehensive report submitted to WordCamp Central, shared with sponsors, and published for the community — required within 1 month of the event (see post-event-workflow).
