# Post-Event

Everything from the 48-hour thank-yous to the 1-month financial closeout.

### [Post-Event Workflow](post-event/post-event-workflow)
The post-event period matters for keeping community momentum going and improving future events. This workflow makes sure nothing gets missed between the last attendee leaving and the team formally closing out the event.

### [Post-Event Report Guide](post-event/event-report-guide)
The structure for the full report submitted to WordCamp Central, shared with sponsors, and published for the community. Due within 1 month of the event (see post-event-workflow).
