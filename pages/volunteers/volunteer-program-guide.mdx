# Volunteer Program Guide

**Purpose:** What volunteering at WCPHC actually involves — roles, shifts, training, and what volunteers receive. For how volunteers are recruited and the application form, see [recruiting/volunteer-recruitment-sop.md](../recruiting/volunteer-recruitment-sop).

**Owner:** Volunteer Coordinator

## Volunteer roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Registration Desk | Check in attendees, distribute badges and swag |
| Stage Support | Assist speakers with setup, manage microphones |
| Speaker Support | Guide speakers, manage green room |
| Media Team | Photography, videography, social media coverage |
| Crowd Control | Manage queues, assist attendees |
| Technical Support | WiFi assistance, AV troubleshooting |
| Setup/Teardown | Venue setup before and cleanup after the event |
| Any role needed | Flexible assignment, placed where coverage is thinnest |

Detailed event-day station responsibilities: [event-day/operations-runbook.md](../event-day/operations-runbook) "Key Stations & Responsibilities."

## Shifts

- Setup Day (day before event)
- Event Day – Morning Shift
- Event Day – Afternoon Shift
- Event Day – Full Day
- Teardown (day after event)

Volunteers indicate availability at application time; shift assignment is finalized 2 months before the event (see [planning/master-planning-timeline.md](../planning/master-planning-timeline)).

## What volunteers receive

- A WordCamp t-shirt (sizes S/M/L/XL/XXL, collected at application)
- Meals during their shift (dietary restrictions collected at application)
- Certificate of participation
- Access to the full event

## Mandatory training

A volunteer training session is held **1 month before the event** (non-negotiable per the master timeline). Training should cover:
- Their assigned role and station responsibilities
- The communication system for event day (see [event-day/operations-runbook.md](../event-day/operations-runbook) "Communication System")
- The issue-response playbook basics ([event-day/issue-response-playbook.md](../event-day/issue-response-playbook)) — who to call for power, internet, medical, or complaint escalations
- Code of Conduct expectations — see [policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety.md](../policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety)

Training Completion Rate is tracked as a KPI — a volunteer who hasn't completed training should not be assigned an unsupervised station on event day.

## Volunteer commitment

Volunteers agree, at application, to show up on time for their assigned shift and to communicate promptly if they cannot fulfill their commitment. If a confirmed volunteer needs to drop out, they should notify the Volunteer Coordinator as early as possible so a role isn't left uncovered — see [organisation/accountability-and-escalation.md](../organisation/accountability-and-escalation).

## KPIs

| KPI | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Volunteers Confirmed | 40+ | Confirmed volunteers on dashboard |
| Coverage by Role | Every role staffed | Dashboard grouped by role |
| Training Completion Rate | 100% before event day | Training attendance log |
| Shift Coverage | Every shift fully staffed | Dashboard grouped by shift |

Dashboard views to maintain: All Volunteers, By Role, By Shift, By Status. See [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup).

## Post-event

Volunteer feedback is collected within 1 week (see [post-event/post-event-workflow.md](../post-event/post-event-workflow)) and volunteer performance is assessed at the 2-week internal review — strong volunteers are a priority re-recruitment pool for the following year's organizing team.
