# Event Day Operations Runbook

**Purpose:** Step-by-step instructions for running event day. Share this document with every organizer and team lead — everyone on-site should know what happens when, and who is where. For what to do when something goes wrong, see [event-day/issue-response-playbook.md](issue-response-playbook).

**Owner:** Lead Organizer, executed by all Team Leads.

## Command center

Designate a central, findable location as the command center for rapid decision-making. Access is limited to:
- Lead Organizer
- Logistics Lead
- Volunteer Coordinator
- Program/Speakers Lead

Any issue that can't be resolved at the station level (see [issue-response-playbook.md](issue-response-playbook)) comes here.

## Communication system

Set up before the event, tested during setup day:

| Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Organizers Group | Core team decisions |
| Volunteers Group | Volunteer coordination |
| Emergency Group | Urgent issues only — keep this channel quiet outside real emergencies so it stays trustworthy |
| Speaker Group | Speaker communications |

## Setup Timeline

### Day before event (Setup Day)
☐ Venue access confirmed
☐ Deliver and set up sponsor booths/displays
☐ Test all AV equipment
☐ Set up registration desk
☐ Place directional signage
☐ Prepare swag bags
☐ Test WiFi connectivity
☐ Confirm generator fuel levels

### Event Day — Morning (6:00 AM – 8:00 AM)
☐ 6:00 AM — Lead organizer arrives, unlocks venue
☐ 6:30 AM — AV team arrives, sets up equipment
☐ 7:00 AM — Registration team arrives, sets up desk
☐ 7:15 AM — Catering arrives, sets up breakfast
☐ 7:30 AM — All volunteers arrive for briefing
☐ 7:45 AM — Volunteer briefing and role assignments
☐ 8:00 AM — Doors open for registration

### Event Day — During Event
☐ Registration desk staffed at all times
☐ Stage manager coordinates speaker transitions
☐ AV technician monitors all rooms
☐ Volunteers assist attendees and manage queues
☐ Social media team posts updates and photos — see [marketing-and-communications/social-media-playbook.md](../marketing-and-communications/social-media-playbook)
☐ Photographer/videographer captures sessions
☐ Logistics team monitors facilities and catering

## Key stations & responsibilities

### Registration Desk
Check in attendees against the registration list, distribute badges and swag bags, handle walk-in registrations if applicable, answer general questions, direct attendees to session rooms.

### Stage Control
Introduce speakers, manage session timing, coordinate Q&A sessions, handle technical issues during presentations, signal time remaining to speakers.

### Speaker Prep Room
Welcome arriving speakers, provide water and refreshments, assist with AV setup, escort speakers to stage, collect speaker feedback.

### Sponsor Booth Area
Ensure sponsors have what they need, monitor booth traffic, coordinate sponsor speaking slots (where applicable, per [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance)), collect sponsor feedback.

### Media Coverage
Capture photos of sessions, attendees, and networking; record video of keynotes (if approved); post real-time updates to social media; interview attendees for testimonials.

## End of day procedures

☐ Thank attendees and speakers

☐ Secure all equipment
☐ Coordinate sponsor teardown

☐ Lock venue and set alarm
☐ Debrief with organizing team
