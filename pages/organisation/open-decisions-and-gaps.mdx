# Open Decisions & Gaps

**Purpose:** A living list of things the team has not yet formally decided, so silence on a topic is never mistaken for an answer. Per [organisation/accountability-and-escalation.md](accountability-and-escalation), an undecided item still needs an owner and a deadline — the owner here is "decide this," not "execute this."

When a decision below gets made, move it into the relevant playbook or policy document and delete the row here — don't leave resolved items in this list.

| Open question | Why it matters | Suggested owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who formally ratifies and enforces the Code of Conduct, and what is the reporting/response process for a violation? | WordCamp Central requires events be open to all; a COC without a named enforcer is not enforceable. Draft baseline exists at [policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety.md](../policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety) | Lead Organizer | Needs decision |
| What is the approved budget template and who approves spending above a set threshold? | Financial transparency is a WordCamp Central requirement; current material gives targets (₦10M sponsorship goal, break-even) but no spending-approval workflow | Lead Organizer + Logistics Lead | Needs decision |
| What is the ticket refund / cancellation policy? | Not addressed anywhere in source material; will be asked by attendees during registration | Marketing & Communications Lead | Needs decision |
| Who is the backup owner for each Team Lead if they become unavailable? | Prevents single points of failure per [00-start-here/welcome-and-philosophy.md](../00-start-here/welcome-and-philosophy) volunteer-driven principle | Each Team Lead, confirmed by Lead Organizer | Needs decision per team |
| What accessibility accommodations does WCPHC commit to beyond venue wheelchair access (sign language interpretation, quiet room, dietary coverage beyond vegetarian/vegan)? | Checklist currently only covers physical accessibility of the venue | Logistics Lead | Needs decision |
| What data is collected at registration/volunteer/organizer sign-up, how long is it kept, and who can access it? | Application forms collect phone numbers, emergency contacts, and dietary/health-adjacent data; no retention or access policy exists | Lead Organizer | Needs decision |
| What is the sponsor conflict-of-interest or category-exclusivity policy (e.g., can two competing hosting companies both sponsor)? | Not addressed in sponsorship material | Sponsorship Lead | Needs decision |
| At what point does Partnerships & Outreach split into two separate teams (see [organisation/team-structure-and-roles.md](team-structure-and-roles) consolidation note)? | Currently one team covers four segments; this works at current scale but may not at 25+ partnerships | Lead Organizer | Monitor, revisit if team grows |
| Who has final sign-off on venue selection and contract signing? | Timeline checklist has "confirm venue and sign contract" at 8 months but names no approver | Lead Organizer | Needs decision |
| What is the process for a sponsor or partner requesting something outside standard tier benefits? | Tiers are fixed in reference tables; no exception process is defined | Sponsorship Lead / Partnerships Lead | Needs decision |

## How to add to this list

If you hit a decision that isn't yours to make alone and isn't covered anywhere in the documentation, add a row here during your next weekly report rather than making a local, undocumented call. See [reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide.md](../reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide) "Items needing escalation."
