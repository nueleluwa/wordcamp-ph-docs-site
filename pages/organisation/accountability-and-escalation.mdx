# Accountability & Escalation

**Purpose:** WCPHC's accountability culture, made operational. This is the page every other document points to when it asks "what happens if I can't complete a task?"

## The rule

Every task tracked anywhere in WCPHC's operation — a dashboard card, a checklist item, a deliverable in a playbook — must have three things before it's considered "assigned":

1. **A named person**, not a team. "Logistics" cannot own a task; a person on the Logistics team can.
2. **A clear deadline.** "Soon" or "before the event" is not a deadline.
3. **A progress-check mechanism** — a dashboard field, a weekly report line, or a standing meeting agenda item that surfaces the task's status without anyone having to ask.

If a task in any checklist or dashboard is missing one of these three, that is a defect to fix immediately, not a detail to fill in later.

## Why this exists

Organizers and volunteers are unpaid ([00-start-here/welcome-and-philosophy.md](../00-start-here/welcome-and-philosophy)). A task that depends on someone "just remembering" quietly becomes a task that depends on whoever is most anxious about letting the team down — which is how burnout happens. A named owner, a deadline, and a visible progress check replace memory and guilt with a system.

## Escalation ladder

Use this when you're blocked — don't sit on a blocker hoping it resolves itself.

| Situation | Escalate to | Where |
|---|---|---|
| You can't complete a task by its deadline | Your Team Lead | Weekly report "Blockers" section, or immediately if urgent — see [reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide.md](../reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide) |
| A cross-team dependency is stuck (e.g. Marketing needs sponsor logos Sponsorship hasn't collected) | Both Team Leads, in the weekly organizer meeting | [planning/weekly-operating-rhythm.md](../planning/weekly-operating-rhythm) |
| A team-level KPI is off track and the team doesn't have a fix | Lead Organizer | Weekly report "Items needing escalation" |
| An event-day issue | Follow the issue-response table | [event-day/issue-response-playbook.md](../event-day/issue-response-playbook) |
| A decision nobody has authority to make alone (budget reallocation, policy exception) | Lead Organizer, logged as an open decision | [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](open-decisions-and-gaps) |
| A safety, harassment, or Code of Conduct issue | Immediate escalation to Lead Organizer regardless of channel | [policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety.md](../policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety) |

## What "can't complete a task" should never mean

It should never mean silently missing the deadline and hoping no one notices. Missing a deadline is normal in volunteer-run events — the failure mode this system exists to prevent is the deadline being missed **invisibly**. Say so, as early as possible, in your weekly report or directly to your Team Lead.

## Progress-check mechanisms in use

- **Dashboards** (Notion or equivalent) — real-time, for anything with a count or status. See [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup).
- **Weekly reports** — for narrative status, blockers, and wins. See [reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide.md](../reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide).
- **Standing meeting agenda items** — for anything that needs a live decision. See [planning/weekly-operating-rhythm.md](../planning/weekly-operating-rhythm).

Do not invent a fourth mechanism (a side WhatsApp thread, a private note) for tracking a real task — if it's not in one of the three above, it isn't actually being tracked.
