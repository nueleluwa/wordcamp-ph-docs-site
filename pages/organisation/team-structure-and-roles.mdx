# Team Structure & Roles

**Purpose:** One page to answer "which team am I on, what does that team own, and who else is on it."

## Org shape

WCPHC is run by a **Lead Organizer** and a set of **Team Leads**, each owning one functional area end to end (strategy, execution, reporting). Team Leads recruit and coordinate their own team members. No team should have a single point of failure. Every Team Lead should have at least one other person who can run their function if they're unavailable.

```
Lead Organizer
 ├── Sponsorship Lead
 ├── Partnerships & Outreach Lead
 ├── Marketing & Communications Lead (Design sits within this team)
 ├── Program & Speakers Lead
 ├── Volunteer Coordinator
 └── Logistics Lead
```

## Teams and what they own

| Team | Owns | Primary playbook |
|---|---|---|
| **Sponsorship** | Sponsor prospecting, outreach, negotiation, revenue target, fulfillment | [sponsorship/](../sponsorship/) |
| **Partnerships & Outreach** | Relationships with universities, training hubs, corporates, co-working spaces, and tech communities; student/professional pipeline into registration | [partnerships-and-outreach/](../partnerships-and-outreach/) |
| **Marketing & Communications** | Social media, website content, articles, email campaigns, announcements, design assets | [marketing-and-communications/](../marketing-and-communications/) |
| **Program & Speakers** | Call for Speakers, review, schedule creation | [speakers-and-program/](../speakers-and-program/) |
| **Volunteer Coordination** | Volunteer recruitment, training, shift scheduling, event-day coordination | [volunteers/](../volunteers/) |
| **Logistics** | Venue, power backup, catering, signage, registration desk, vendor contracts | [venue-and-logistics/](../venue-and-logistics/) |

> **Consolidation note:** the source planning material sometimes referred to "Outreach" (schools, training hubs, corporates, communities) and "Partnerships" as separate efforts. This documentation treats them as one team, **Partnerships & Outreach**, with four internal segment owners (Schools, Training Hubs, Corporate/Co-working, Communities). In practice, the same relationship-building skills and pipeline mechanics apply to all four, and attendees don't care which internal team recruited them. If the team later grows large enough to justify splitting Outreach (student-focused) from Partnerships (organizational) into two full teams with separate leads, update this page and the org chart above. Don't let the split happen informally.

## Skill areas (used for organizer recruitment matching)

When recruiting, match applicants against these skill areas. See [recruiting/organizer-recruitment-sop.md](../recruiting/organizer-recruitment-sop):

- Sponsorship: prospecting, outreach, relationship management
- Partnerships & Outreach: universities, training hubs, startup hubs, corporates, communities
- Marketing: social media, website, email campaigns, announcements
- Design: graphics, banners, promotional materials (supports Marketing)
- Logistics: venue coordination, power backup, catering, signage, registration desk
- Program/Speakers: Call for Speakers, review, schedule
- Volunteer Coordination: recruitment and event-day coordination

## Every role, not just team leads

Team Leads own outcomes. They aren't expected to do all the work personally. Each team should have named members against specific sub-responsibilities (e.g., within Logistics: one person owns power/generator, another owns catering). If a sub-responsibility inside a team has no named owner, log it. See [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](open-decisions-and-gaps).
