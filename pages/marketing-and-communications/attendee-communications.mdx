# Attendee Communications

**Purpose:** The direct, transactional emails sent to individual attendees, as distinct from broadcast social/content marketing. Owner: Marketing & Communications Lead, in coordination with Logistics for venue/parking details.

## Registration confirmation

**Subject:** Your WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year] Registration

> Hello [Name],
>
> Thank you for registering for WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]! We're excited to have you join us.
>
> **EVENT DETAILS:**
> Date: [Date]
> Time: [Time]
> Venue: [Venue Address]
>
> **WHAT'S NEXT:**
> - Save the date in your calendar
> - Follow us on social media for updates: [Links]
> - Join our community: [Link]
>
> If you have any questions, please reply to this email.
>
> See you at WordCamp!
> The WordCamp Port Harcourt Team

## Open item: ticket policy

Neither template above addresses refunds, cancellations, or transfers. Until a policy is set, don't promise anything about cancellations in ad-hoc replies to attendees — escalate the question. See [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](../organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps).
