# Team Advocacy & Sharing

> **What this is:** Why organizers and volunteers should talk about WordCamp Port Harcourt in their own networks, what to share, and the rules for doing it.
>
> **What to do next:** Marketing builds the weekly share pack. Every organizer and volunteer joins the sharing group and posts from it.

**Purpose:** The official WordCamp accounts only reach people who already follow them. Organizers and volunteers reach everyone else. This page sets out why that matters, what we ask of the team, and how to keep it accurate and safe. Channels, cadence and post templates live in [marketing-and-communications/social-media-playbook.md](social-media-playbook).

**Owner:** Marketing & Communications Lead

## Why this matters

| Need | What it means for WCPHC |
|---|---|
| **Reach beyond our followers** | The social reach target is 20,000. A new event account starts with a small audience, and each team member is a channel with their own contacts. |
| **Trust** | People in Port Harcourt buy tickets and sponsor events on a personal recommendation. A message from someone they know carries more weight than an event page post. |
| **The channels people actually use** | Much of the local conversation happens on WhatsApp groups, status updates and direct messages. The official accounts can't reach those. Team members can. |
| **Ticket sales and the student mix** | The plan needs about 750 attendees, including 250 students. Students and first-time attendees mostly hear about events through friends, lecturers and campus groups. |
| **Sponsor value** | Sponsors are promised reach and visibility. More shares of posts that tag them make that promise real. |
| **Volunteer and speaker pipeline** | Public enthusiasm from the team is what draws next year's volunteers and speakers. |
| **Signal of a healthy event** | A team that visibly cares tells outsiders the event is worth their time and money. |

## What we ask of the team

Everyone on the organizing team and every confirmed volunteer is asked to do three small things:

1. **Join the sharing group** (a WhatsApp group run by Marketing) and read it weekly.
2. **Share at least one official post a week** during active promotion, from the pack Marketing prepares. Sharing means reposting, quote-posting with a personal line, or forwarding to a relevant WhatsApp group.
3. **Say one true, personal thing** now and then: why you are involved, what you are looking forward to, or a behind-the-scenes moment.

Nobody is required to post anything on a personal account they don't want to use. Sharing is encouraged, not enforced, and it is never a condition of volunteering.

## What to share

| Content | Where it comes from | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Ticket links and early-bird deadlines | Official accounts | WhatsApp groups, status, direct messages |
| Speaker announcements | Official accounts, after the speaker is announced publicly | Reposts with a personal comment |
| Sponsor thank-yous | Official accounts | Tagging the sponsor, which boosts their reach too |
| Student ticket and volunteer calls | Official accounts | Campus groups, community groups |
| Behind-the-scenes (planning meetings, venue visits, packing swag) | Team members, cleared with Marketing if it shows sponsors or unannounced details | Personal accounts |
| Event-day moments | Media Team and attendees | Live posts, stories, tagged #WordCampPH |
| Post-event recap, photos and thank-yous | Official accounts | Reposts, tagging people in photos |

## Sharing rhythm by phase

This follows the content calendar in the [Social Media Playbook](social-media-playbook).

| Phase | Ask of the team |
|---|---|
| Announcement (9 months before) | Repost the save-the-date. Tell your own network why you are involved. |
| Early bird (4 months before) | Forward the ticket link to at least three relevant groups. |
| Speaker reveal (3 months before) | Repost each speaker card and tag the speaker. |
| Countdown (1 month before) | Share the countdown posts. Remind friends of the last-chance deadline. |
| Event day | Post a photo or story with #WordCampPH. Reshare official posts. |
| Post-event (1 to 2 weeks after) | Share the recap, tag people in photos, thank sponsors and speakers publicly. |

## How Marketing supports the team

- **A weekly share pack** in the sharing group: the week's official posts, ready-to-forward text, and image links.
- **A short caption bank**, so no one has to write from scratch, plus room to add a personal line.
- **Always use the hashtag #WordCampPH**, with the others from the playbook where they fit.
- **A single place for corrections.** If something is posted wrongly, Marketing posts the fix there.
- **Recognition** for the most helpful sharers. See "Recognising sharers" below.

## Recognising sharers

Sharing is voluntary, so recognition is a thank-you, not a payment. Three forms:

1. **Public thank-you.** Name the top sharers in the sharing group, in the post-event thank-you post, and in the post-event report.
2. **Digital certificate.** Marketing issues a "WordCamp Port Harcourt Community Ambassador" certificate, and the Lead Organizer writes a short LinkedIn recommendation on request. Students and early-career volunteers often value this most.
3. **Swag.** A small branded item (stickers, notebook or lanyard) handed over at the team gathering or event day. Use items already in the swag budget, or ask a swag sponsor to supply them as an in-kind gift. See [sponsorship/in-kind-sponsorship.md](../sponsorship/in-kind-sponsorship).

**How sharers are chosen:** by quality and consistency, not post count. Criteria are stated upfront in the sharing group. Referrals from the "Heard about us from" question are one input, not the only one.

**What we don't do:** pay cash or per-referral rewards, give away tickets or speaker-dinner places as prizes, tie anything to a speaking slot, or publish a leaderboard.

## Rules for sharing

<Callout type="warning">
  **Do not:** announce speakers, sponsors or programme details before the official accounts do. Share anyone's registration details. Sell or promise speaking slots. See [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance).
</Callout>

- **The official accounts are the source of truth.** Share their dates, prices and links. If you're unsure, ask Marketing instead of guessing.
- **Say who you are.** On a personal account, make clear the views are your own. Write that you are an organizer or volunteer where it is relevant.
- **Be honest.** No claims about attendance, speakers or sponsors that aren't confirmed.
- **Respect the code of conduct.** Be welcoming in public conversations and don't argue with critics online. Pass concerns to Marketing. See [policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety.md](../policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety).
- **Ask before posting other people.** Don't post photos of people who've asked not to be shown, and never post minors without a guardian's agreement.
- **Don't spam.** Forward the ticket link to relevant groups once, not repeatedly. Check the group's rules first.
- **Sponsor visibility.** Tag sponsors when sharing their posts. Don't promote one sponsor over another, or promote a sponsor's competitors.

## Measuring it

Track simply, so it doesn't become another chore:

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Team sharing rate | Count of team members who shared at least once per month (a tally in the sharing group) |
| Reach | Social Media Reach against the 20,000 target. See [reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework.md](../reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework). |
| Ticket source | Add "Heard about us from" to registration, with an option for "A WordCamp team member or volunteer" |
| Hashtag use | Posts tagged #WordCampPH, counted weekly |

Report these in the post-event report. See [post-event/event-report-guide.md](../post-event/event-report-guide).

## Common risks

| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Team posts early and leaks an unannounced speaker | Share only what the official accounts have posted. Marketing reminds the group before each announcement. |
| Sharing fatigue | Keep the ask to one post a week and stop after the event. |
| Inconsistent facts | One share pack, one source of truth. |
| A volunteer feels pressured | Sharing is voluntary. Make that explicit when recruiting. |
| Personal accounts attract criticism | Don't respond publicly. Escalate to Marketing. |

## Open decisions

- Who runs the sharing group and prepares the weekly pack (proposed: the Marketing & Communications Lead, with one named volunteer).
- Whether to add a "Heard about us from" question to registration. This needs Registration to agree.
- Whether the swag for sharers comes from the swag budget or an in-kind swag sponsor.
