# Social Media Playbook

**Purpose:** Templates and cadence for social media across the event lifecycle. For long-form content (articles, blog posts), see [marketing-and-communications/content-and-articles-guide.md](content-and-articles-guide). For direct attendee emails, see [marketing-and-communications/attendee-communications.md](attendee-communications).

**Owner:** Marketing & Communications Lead

## Channels

| Channel | Role |
|---|---|
| Twitter/X | Primary channel for real-time updates |
| LinkedIn | Professional networking and B2B outreach |
| Instagram | Visual content and community highlights |
| Facebook | Community group and event page |
| WhatsApp | Direct community communication |

## Hashtag strategy

Use consistently across all platforms: **#WordCampPH** (primary), **#WordCamp**, **#WordPress**, **#PortHarcourt**, **#TechPH**.

## Content calendar framework

| Phase | Timing | Content focus |
|---|---|---|
| Announcement | 9 months before | Save the date, theme reveal |
| Early Bird | 4 months before | Ticket sales open, early bird pricing |
| Speaker Reveal | 3 months before | Speaker announcements, session previews |
| Countdown | 1 month before | Daily countdown, last-chance messaging |
| Live Event | Event day | Real-time updates, photos, highlights |
| Post-Event | 1–2 weeks after | Recap, thank-yous, content sharing |

This lines up with the master timeline. See [planning/master-planning-timeline.md](../planning/master-planning-timeline).

**Announcement:**
> Exciting news! WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year] is coming on [Date]! Join us for a day of learning, networking, and community building. Save the date and stay tuned for more details! #WordCampPH #WordPress #PortHarcourt

**Ticket sales open:**
> Tickets are now on sale for WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]! Early bird pricing available for a limited time. Don't miss this opportunity to learn from industry experts and connect with the WordPress community. Get your ticket: [Link] #WordCampPH

**Speaker announcement:**
> We're thrilled to announce [Speaker Name] as a speaker at WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]! [He/She/They] will be presenting on "[Talk Title]." You won't want to miss this session! Get your ticket: [Link] #WordCampPH #SpeakerSpotlight

> Only [X] days until WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]! Have you got your ticket yet? Join us on [Date] at [Venue] for an amazing day of WordPress learning and community. Last chance tickets: [Link] #WordCampPH #Countdown

## Best practices

- **Post consistently**: at least 3–5 posts a week during active promotion periods
- **Use visuals**: include images or graphics with every post when possible
- **Engage with comments**: respond to questions and comments promptly
- **Tag relevant accounts**: tag speakers, sponsors, and partners to increase reach
- **Monitor hashtags**: track #WordCampPH and related tags
- **Share user-generated content**: repost attendee photos and testimonials
- **Be authentic**: let the community voice come through
