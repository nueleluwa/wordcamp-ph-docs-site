# Content & Articles Guide

**Purpose:** Frameworks for longer-form content (blog posts, articles) that builds community engagement year-round, not just around the event. For social posts, see [marketing-and-communications/social-media-playbook.md](social-media-playbook).

**Owner:** Marketing & Communications Lead

## Content strategy

Content serves the community flywheel: **Community → Content → Events → Contributors → Sponsors → More Community.** Every piece of content should move someone through this cycle. If a proposed article doesn't clearly advance one of these links, reconsider it.

## Article types

### 1. Speaker Spotlight
Purpose: introduce speakers and build excitement for their sessions.
Structure: Introduction (who they are and why they're notable) → Background (their journey with WordPress/tech) → Session Preview (what they'll talk about) → Key Takeaway (what attendees will learn) → Call to Action (get your ticket).

### 2. Sponsor Highlight
Purpose: showcase sponsors and explain why they support the community.
Structure: Introduction (company, what they do) → Why WordPress (their connection to the ecosystem) → Why Sponsor (why they chose WCPHC) → What They Offer (relevant products/services) → Call to Action (visit their booth/website).

### 3. Community Member Story
Purpose: humanize the community and show diverse paths into WordPress.
Structure: Introduction (who this person is) → Their Journey (how they got started) → Challenges (obstacles overcome) → Success (what they've achieved) → Advice (tips for others) → Call to Action (join the community).

### 4. Event Recap
Purpose: document the event and extend its impact beyond attendees.
Structure: Overview (stats) → Highlights (key moments, quotes) → Speaker Summaries → Community (networking moments) → Photos → Thank You (sponsors, speakers, volunteers) → Looking Ahead (tease next year).
This is the public-facing counterpart to the internal [post-event/event-report-guide.md](../post-event/event-report-guide).

### 5. Educational Content
Purpose: provide value year-round and establish thought leadership.
Topics: WordPress tutorials and tips, freelancing and business advice, security best practices, performance optimization, design trends, SEO and marketing strategies.

## Content calendar template

| Week | Blog post | Social posts | Email |
|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Speaker Spotlight #1 | 3 posts | Newsletter |
| Week 2 | Sponsor Highlight #1 | 3 posts | – |
| Week 3 | Community Story #1 | 3 posts | – |
| Week 4 | Educational Article | 3 posts | Monthly Roundup |

Repeat this four-week cycle throughout the active promotion period, adjusting article subjects to match confirmed speakers/sponsors as they're locked in.

## Writing guidelines

- Write for your audience: developers, designers, business owners, and beginners all read differently
- Be inclusive: use gender-neutral language and avoid unexplained jargon
- Be authentic: let community voices come through directly, in quotes and interviews
- Include visuals: photos, screenshots, infographics
- Optimize for SEO: use relevant keywords naturally, don't force them
- Include calls to action: guide readers to a next step
- Proofread before publishing

## Publishing platforms

WordCamp Website (official announcements), WordPress.org News (community-wide reach), Medium (broader tech audience), LinkedIn Articles (professional network), Dev.to (developer community), personal blogs (individual organizer perspectives).

## Content repurposing

Maximize value from every piece of content:
- Blog post → social media threads
- Speaker session → blog recap → video clips
- Event photos → Instagram carousel → Facebook album
- Survey results → infographic → social posts
- Community stories → newsletter feature → blog series
