# WordCamp Port Harcourt: Team Documentation

This is the operating manual for WordCamp Port Harcourt (WCPHC): a community-organized WordPress conference for Port Harcourt and the South-South Nigeria region, run entirely by volunteers under WordCamp Central guidelines.

<RoleCards />

Already know your team? Jump straight to your section in the sidebar. It's grouped by how the team actually works, not by when a document was written.

## Document types used throughout

- **Guide / Playbook**: explains a process end to end, covering strategy, steps, ownership, and timing.
- **Checklist**: a sequenced list of actions to complete, in order, with nothing skipped.
- **Template**: a blank, reusable document meant to be copied and filled in repeatedly, like an application form, a weekly report, or a tracking sheet.
- **Policy**: a rule the team must follow, usually because WordCamp Central requires it.
- **Reference**: lookup information, like tiers, contacts, or KPI targets, that doesn't change week to week.

## Core principles (see [welcome-and-philosophy.md](00-start-here/welcome-and-philosophy) for full detail)

1. **Community first**: decisions favor community benefit over individual or organizational gain.
2. **Volunteer-driven**: organizers and volunteers aren't employees, and work is spread out to avoid burnout.
3. **Built for the Nigerian context**: power, internet, traffic, and payment realities are planned for, not treated as edge cases.
4. **Accountability by design**: every task has an owner, a deadline, and a progress-check mechanism. No task should depend on someone "just remembering."

## Keeping this documentation current

This is a living operating system, not an archive. When a process changes, update the relevant document in the same change. Don't let the docs drift from what the team actually does. Open questions the team hasn't resolved yet are tracked explicitly in [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps) rather than being silently skipped.
