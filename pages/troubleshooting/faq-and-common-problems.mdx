# FAQ & Common Problems

**Purpose:** Fast answers to the problems that come up repeatedly. If your problem isn't listed here, it may belong in [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](../organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps) — add it during your next weekly report rather than solving it quietly and differently each time.

## "A task has no clear owner"

That's a defect, not a normal situation. See [organisation/accountability-and-escalation.md](../organisation/accountability-and-escalation) — every task needs a named person, a deadline, and a progress-check mechanism. Raise it with your Team Lead immediately rather than doing it yourself without it being tracked.

## "I'm behind on my task and won't hit the deadline"

Say so early, in your weekly report or directly to your Team Lead — see [reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide.md](../reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide). The failure mode this documentation is built to prevent is a missed deadline nobody knew about, not a missed deadline itself.

## "A sponsor is asking for a guaranteed speaking slot"

<Callout type="warning">
  Not allowed under any tier. Redirect them to the standard Call for Speakers process. See [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance) and [speakers-and-program/call-for-speakers-and-program-guide.md](../speakers-and-program/call-for-speakers-and-program-guide).
</Callout>

## "A confirmed volunteer just dropped out close to the event"

Notify the Volunteer Coordinator immediately so the shift can be reassigned — see [volunteers/volunteer-program-guide.md](../volunteers/volunteer-program-guide) and [recruiting/volunteer-recruitment-sop.md](../recruiting/volunteer-recruitment-sop). Don't quietly ask an organizer to absorb the shift without it being visible — that undermines the anti-burnout principle in [00-start-here/welcome-and-philosophy.md](../00-start-here/welcome-and-philosophy).

## "The power/internet/AV failed during the event"

Follow the response table directly: [event-day/issue-response-playbook.md](../event-day/issue-response-playbook). If it's not resolved by the named contact, escalate to the command center — [event-day/operations-runbook.md](../event-day/operations-runbook).

## "A prospect (sponsor or partner) has gone quiet after the follow-up sequence"

Mark them closed-lost on the dashboard rather than leaving the record open indefinitely — see [sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook.md](../sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook) or the relevant [partnerships-and-outreach/](../partnerships-and-outreach/) segment playbook. A stale "In Discussion" record makes the pipeline view unreliable for the whole team.

## "I don't know which team owns this"

Check [organisation/team-structure-and-roles.md](../organisation/team-structure-and-roles) first. If it genuinely doesn't fit any team, raise it at the weekly organizer meeting — see [planning/weekly-operating-rhythm.md](../planning/weekly-operating-rhythm) — rather than letting it sit unowned.

## "An attendee is asking about refunds/cancellations"

<Callout type="warning">
  There is currently no adopted policy — don't promise anything. Escalate to the Lead Organizer. See [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](../organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps).
</Callout>

## "Someone reported harassment or a safety concern"

<Callout type="error">
  This does not go through the normal complaint-handling path. Escalate immediately and directly to the Lead Organizer. See [policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety.md](../policies/code-of-conduct-and-safety).
</Callout>

## "The dashboard numbers don't match what I know to be true"

Someone likely missed a Friday update. See [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup) "Keeping the dashboard trustworthy" — fix the record, then check whether the owning team's weekly reporting has lapsed.

## "This documentation is out of date / wrong"

Fix it in the same change that surfaces the problem — don't work around outdated documentation and leave it wrong for the next person. See [README.md](..) "Keeping this documentation current."
