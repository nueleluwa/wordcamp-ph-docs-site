# Sponsorship Outreach Playbook

**Purpose:** The step-by-step process and email templates for moving a sponsor prospect from cold contact to confirmed. Tier reference: [sponsorship/sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers.md](sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers). Post-confirmation steps: [sponsorship/sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.md](sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment).

**Owner:** Sponsorship Lead and team members assigned prospects on the dashboard.

## Cadence

Target: **20 new contacts per week**, starting at 8 months before the event and continuing through 3 months before. Weekly rhythm: Tuesday sends, Friday reporting. See [planning/weekly-operating-rhythm.md](../planning/weekly-operating-rhythm).

## Outreach sequence

| Step | Timing | Template |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Initial outreach | Day 0 | Template 1 below |
| 2. Follow-up | Day 5 | Template 2 below |
| 3. Second follow-up ("last call") | Day 10 | Template 3 below |
| 4. Confirmation | On yes | Template 4 below → hand off to [sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.md](sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment) |

If no response after Template 3, mark the prospect as closed-lost on the dashboard rather than leaving it open indefinitely. A stale "In Discussion" record makes the pipeline view unreliable for everyone else.

## Template 1: Initial Outreach

**Subject:** Partner with WordCamp Port Harcourt: [Year]

> Hello [Contact Name],
>
> I hope this email finds you well. My name is [Your Name], and I'm part of the organizing team for WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year], a community-driven event that brings together WordPress users, developers, designers, and businesses from across Nigeria's South-South region.
>
> WordCamp Port Harcourt provides a unique opportunity for organizations to connect with a vibrant tech community while supporting open-source learning and professional development. Last year's event attracted [X] attendees, and we're expecting even more this year.
>
> We would love to explore a sponsorship partnership with [Company Name]. Your support would help us:
> - Provide affordable tickets to students and emerging professionals
> - Bring quality speakers and workshops to the community
> - Create networking opportunities for local developers and businesses
>
> In return, we offer valuable visibility and engagement opportunities with our tech-savvy audience.
>
> I've attached our sponsorship deck with detailed information about partnership tiers and benefits. Would you be available for a brief call next week to discuss how we can work together?
>
> Thank you for considering this opportunity to support the WordPress community in Port Harcourt.
>
> Warm regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Your Title], WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]
> [Your Email] | [Your Phone]

## Template 2: Follow-Up (5 days after initial)

**Subject:** Following Up: WordCamp Port Harcourt Partnership

> Hello [Contact Name],
>
> I wanted to follow up on my email from last week regarding a sponsorship opportunity with WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year].
>
> I understand you receive many partnership requests, so I wanted to make sure this didn't get lost in your inbox. Our sponsorship slots are filling up, and I would hate for [Company Name] to miss this opportunity to connect with Port Harcourt's WordPress community.
>
> To recap, WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year] will be held on [Date] at [Venue], expecting [X] attendees including developers, designers, agency owners, and digital professionals.
>
> Would you have 15 minutes this week for a quick call? I'm happy to answer any questions and discuss how we can tailor a partnership package that meets your goals.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Your Title], WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]
> [Your Email] | [Your Phone]

## Template 3: Second Follow-Up / Last Call (10 days after initial)

**Subject:** Last Call: WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year] Sponsorship

> Hello [Contact Name],
>
> I hope you're doing well. I wanted to reach out one final time regarding the WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year] sponsorship opportunity.
>
> We're finalizing our sponsor lineup this week, and I wanted to give [Company Name] one last chance to secure a partnership slot before we close applications.
>
> If now isn't the right time for [Company Name] to partner with us, I completely understand. I would appreciate a quick response either way so I can update our records.
>
> Thank you for your time, and I hope we can work together at a future WordCamp.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]
> [Your Title], WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]
> [Your Email] | [Your Phone]

## Template 4: Sponsor Confirmation

**Subject:** Welcome to WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]: [Sponsor Tier] Sponsor

> Hello [Contact Name],
>
> We're thrilled to welcome [Company Name] as a [Sponsor Tier] sponsor for WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]!
>
> Thank you for supporting the WordPress community in Port Harcourt. Your partnership helps us create an inclusive, educational event for developers and digital professionals across the South-South region.
>
> **NEXT STEPS:**
> 1. **Invoice:** Please find attached the invoice for ₦[Amount]. Payment can be made via bank transfer to: Account Name: [Account Name] | Bank: [Bank Name] | Account Number: [Account Number]
> 2. **Logo:** Please send us your company logo in high resolution (PNG or SVG) for use on our website and promotional materials.
> 3. **Sponsor contact:** Please confirm the best contact person for sponsor communications.
> 4. **Booth setup (if applicable):** We'll send specifications and setup instructions closer to the event date.
> 5. **Speaker slot (Platinum only):** If you've opted for a speaking slot, please send your proposed topic and speaker bio by [Deadline].
>
> I've also attached our sponsorship agreement for your records. If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to reach out. We're excited to have you on board!
>
> Warm regards,
> [Your Name]
> Sponsorship Team Lead, WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]
> [Your Email] | [Your Phone]

This confirmation email is where fulfillment begins. Continue to [sponsorship/sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.md](sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment).

Every prospect and its current stage lives on the Sponsorship dashboard, not in this document or in personal notes. See [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup).
