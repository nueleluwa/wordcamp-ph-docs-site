# In-Kind Sponsorship

> **What this is:** The rules for accepting goods and services instead of cash: what counts, how it is valued, what recognition it earns, and how it is recorded.
>
> **What to do next:** When a prospect offers goods or services, work through "Handling an in-kind offer" and fill in the in-kind agreement.

**Purpose:** In-kind sponsors give goods or services the event would otherwise pay for. This page makes those deals consistent, so every in-kind sponsor is valued the same way and the books still reconcile. For the cash tiers, see [sponsorship/sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers.md](sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers).

**Owner:** Sponsorship Lead, with the relevant lead (to confirm the need) and the Lead Organizer (to confirm the value)

<Callout type="info">
  **Settled:** the value bands match WordCamp Central's rules, in-kind can reach Platinum with no cash minimum, the Lead Organizer signs off every in-kind value, and in-kind sponsors do not get category exclusivity. Re-check the current rules with WordCamp Central before the prospectus goes out.
</Callout>

## The principle

An in-kind gift counts toward the sponsorship target only when it **replaces a real budget line**. Value is what we would have paid, not what the sponsor charges its customers. A gift that doesn't reduce a cost we were going to carry is welcome, but it is a donation to thank, not a sponsorship tier.

## What we want

These map to the expense lines in [planning/budget-and-finance-principles.md](../planning/budget-and-finance-principles). Items higher on the list are worth more because they are harder to cover.

| Category | Examples | Budget line it offsets | Owner to confirm need |
|---|---|---|---|
| Venue | Free or discounted hall, breakout rooms, furniture, signage rights | Venue rental | Logistics |
| Power and connectivity | Generator and fuel, backup internet, extension kits | Power backup, Internet | Logistics |
| Food and drink | Lunch, coffee and snacks for attendees, speaker dinner, volunteer meals | Catering, Volunteer meals | Logistics |
| AV and production | Projectors, microphones, screens, livestream or recording | AV equipment rental | Logistics |
| Printing and branding | Badges, lanyards, banners, programme booklets | Printing | Marketing |
| Swag | Tote bags, notebooks, volunteer t-shirts | Swag, Volunteer t-shirts | Marketing |
| Transport | Shuttle, speaker pick-ups, vehicle hire | Transportation | Logistics |
| Services | Photography, videography, design, security | Security, Marketing | Owner of that area |
| Software and tools | Hosting, email or ticketing credits, project tools | Operating costs | Sponsorship Lead |

## What does not count

- **Staff time or volunteers sent by a company.** Welcome, but not valued as sponsorship.
- **Products the event doesn't need**, such as merchandise we would have to store or give away unwanted.
- **Discounts on a service we have not yet decided to buy.** Only count a discount once we commit to the purchase, and then only the saving.
- **Speaking slots.** Never exchanged for any gift. See the hard rule below.
- **Anything with conditions on how we run the event**, such as exclusivity over the programme.

## How value is set

1. **Describe the gift.** What, how much, when, and who delivers it.
2. **Find a comparison price.** Use a written quote from a comparable Port Harcourt supplier, or the sponsor's own price list. Record where the figure came from.
3. **Count only what we would have bought.** If we'd have bought catering for 500 people, a lunch for 800 is valued at 500.
4. **Use the lower figure** where the sponsor's value and the comparison quote differ.
5. **The Lead Organizer confirms the final value** and records it in naira.

## What recognition it earns

Recognition follows the verified value, using the same ladder as cash sponsors.

| Verified value (NGN) | Recognition |
|---|---|
| ₦25,000 to ₦249,999 | Individual-level: sponsors page listing and group thank-you post |
| ₦250,000 to ₦749,999 | Community-level benefits |
| ₦750,000 to ₦1,499,999 | Silver-level benefits |
| ₦1,500,000 to ₦2,999,999 | Gold-level benefits |
| ₦3,000,000 and above | Platinum-level benefits |

Three adjustments apply:

- **Tickets and dinner places are capped.** Cash covers a ticket's cost. An in-kind gift may not, so the full ticket allocation of the matching tier is granted only when the gift offsets a major cost (venue, catering, power, AV). Otherwise ticket counts are reduced and agreed in writing.
- **Recognition is for the thing supplied.** A caterer is credited as "Lunch partner", a printer as "Printing partner", and so on. This is more useful to the sponsor than a generic tier name.
- **Mixed deals are allowed.** A sponsor can pay part in cash and part in kind. Add the two together to find the tier.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Hard rule, not a guideline:** speaking slots are never sold or guaranteed, including in exchange for goods or services. See [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance). Attendee data stays opt-in only, as it does for cash sponsors.
</Callout>

## Handling an in-kind offer

1. **Log it as a prospect** with the type "In-kind" in the pipeline. See [sponsorship/sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers.md](sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers).
2. **Check the need.** Ask the owning lead (see the table above) whether it is a real, budgeted need. If not, politely redirect: offer a smaller cash tier, or thank them as a supporter.
3. **Agree what and when.** Quantity, specification, delivery date and place, and who at the sponsor is responsible.
4. **Value it** using the method above. The Lead Organizer signs off.
5. **Sign the in-kind agreement** (below). Nothing is promised in recognition until this is done.
6. **Confirm** with Template 4 in [sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook.md](sponsorship-outreach-playbook), adjusted to list the goods or services instead of an invoice.
7. **Fulfil** using [sponsorship/sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.md](sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment), skipping the invoice and payment steps.

## The in-kind agreement

One page, signed or confirmed by email by both sides. It records:

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sponsor and contact | Company name, contact person, phone, email |
| Description | What is supplied, in what quantity and to what standard |
| Budget line offset | Which line in the event budget this replaces |
| Agreed value (NGN) | The figure the Lead Organizer confirmed, and how it was found |
| Delivery | Date, time, place and who receives it |
| If not delivered | The fallback: reduced recognition, or the sponsor pays the shortfall in cash |
| Recognition | Tier-equivalent benefits and the partner title |
| Fixed terms | No speaking slot, no attendee data, no programme control |
| Sign-off | Sponsorship Lead, Lead Organizer, and the sponsor |

## Delivery risk

A cash payment arrives or it doesn't. A gift can arrive late, short or wrong. Plan for that:

- **Set the deadline well before the event**, at least 7 days for anything the event can't run without (venue, power, catering, AV).
- **Name a fallback.** For critical items, Logistics keeps a paid alternative on standby until the goods are received.
- **Inspect on arrival.** The owning lead confirms the goods match the agreement and tells the Sponsorship Lead the same day.
- **Adjust recognition to what arrives.** Part delivery earns the tier of the delivered value. Update the dashboard record and tell the sponsor in writing.

## Recording it

In-kind gifts are part of the financial transparency WordCamp Central requires.

- Record the gift as **both revenue and expense** at the agreed value, so budget vs. actual stays accurate. The Lead Organizer confirms the exact treatment.
- Add an **"In-kind" column** to the Revenue Dashboard, so cash and in-kind show separately. See [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup).
- Report in-kind separately in the **financial summary** of the post-event report. See [post-event/event-report-guide.md](../post-event/event-report-guide).
- Keep the signed agreement and the comparison quote with the sponsor's record.

## Messaging to prospects

Lead with the need, not the tier name. Example opening for a local caterer or printer:

> We're looking for a lunch partner for WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]. We expect [X] attendees and need [description]. In return, we would credit [Company Name] as Lunch Partner, with the benefits of our [Tier] sponsorship. We can agree the details in a one-page agreement.

## Decisions made

- The value ladder matches what WordCamp Central allows.
- In-kind deals can reach Platinum. There is no cash minimum.
- The Lead Organizer signs off every in-kind value.
- In-kind sponsors do not get category exclusivity. See [sponsorship/sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers.md](sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers).
