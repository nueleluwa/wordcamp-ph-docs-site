# Sponsor Lifecycle & Fulfillment

**Purpose:** Everything that happens after a sponsor confirms, through the event, to the post-event thank-you. Getting a "yes" is the start of the sponsor relationship, not the end of the task.

**Owner:** Sponsorship Lead

## From confirmation to event day

Triggered by sending Template 4 in [sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook.md](sponsorship-outreach-playbook):

1. Invoice sent with bank transfer details — see [planning/budget-and-finance-principles.md](../planning/budget-and-finance-principles)
2. Payment received and reconciled
3. High-resolution logo collected (PNG or SVG)
4. Sponsor contact person confirmed for ongoing communications
5. Booth specifications sent (if the tier includes booth space) — coordinate with Logistics, see [venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist.md](../venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist)
6. Speaker topic and bio collected by deadline (Platinum only, subject to normal program review — see [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance))
7. Logo added to website and promotional materials (hand off to Marketing — [marketing-and-communications/](../marketing-and-communications/))

Track each step's completion on the sponsor's dashboard record, not as a side checklist — see [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup).

## On event day

Sponsor booths are staffed and monitored as part of the event-day runbook — see [event-day/operations-runbook.md](../event-day/operations-runbook) "Sponsor Booth Area." Any sponsor-reported issue during the event routes through the same escalation path as any other event-day issue — see [event-day/issue-response-playbook.md](../event-day/issue-response-playbook).

> - [X] attendees from across Nigeria's South-South region
> - [X] speakers sharing knowledge on WordPress development, design, and business

>

> Sponsorship Team Lead, WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year]

This is one line item within the broader post-event workflow — see [post-event/post-event-workflow.md](../post-event/post-event-workflow).

## Feeding next year's pipeline

At post-event review (2 weeks out), update the sponsor's dashboard record with renewal likelihood and notes for next year's Sponsorship Lead — see [post-event/post-event-workflow.md](../post-event/post-event-workflow) "Documentation Updates." Do not let this context live only in one organizer's memory.
