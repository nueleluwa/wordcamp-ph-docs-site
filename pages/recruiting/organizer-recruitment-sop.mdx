## When to recruit

Recruitment starts at 8 months before the event (see [planning/master-planning-timeline.md](../planning/master-planning-timeline)) and continues as needed throughout planning. A team with a gap in a skill area (see [organisation/team-structure-and-roles.md](../organisation/team-structure-and-roles)) should recruit against that gap immediately rather than waiting for a scheduled intake.

## Process

1. **Distribute the application form** ([templates/organizer-application-form.md](../templates/organizer-application-form)) through community channels, past-attendee lists, and team member referrals.
2. **Score every applicant** against the weighted rubric below. Two reviewers should score independently before comparing.
3. **Decide:** Shortlisted / Rejected / Waitlist, recorded on the form itself.
4. **Onboard shortlisted applicants**: assign to a team based on their selected skill areas, introduce them to their Team Lead, and add them to the relevant dashboard and communication channels. See [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup).

## Scoring rubric

| Criteria | Weight |
|---|---|
| Community Involvement | 30% |
| Relevant Skills | 25% |
| Availability | 25% |
| Motivation | 20% |

Score each 1–10, multiply by weight, sum for a total weighted score. Use this consistently so applicants across different intake waves are comparable.

## What the form captures

- Personal information and WordPress.org username
- Prior WordCamp/meetup attendance
- Skill areas the applicant can support (maps directly to the teams in [organisation/team-structure-and-roles.md](../organisation/team-structure-and-roles))
- Weekly time availability (2–4, 4–6, 6–10, 10+ hours)
- Motivation (free response)
- Prior event organizing/volunteering experience
- Signed commitment to the volunteer role

## Success looks like

Tracked in the Organizers database on the dashboard: applications received, shortlisted, and placed by team, against each team's headcount need. See [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup).

## If a team can't fill a role

Escalate to the Lead Organizer per [organisation/accountability-and-escalation.md](../organisation/accountability-and-escalation) rather than leaving the responsibility unassigned. An unfilled team role is exactly the kind of ownership gap the accountability system exists to surface early.
