## Target and timing

- Target: 40+ confirmed volunteers (see [reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework.md](../reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework))
- Recruitment plan created: 6 months before
- Recruitment opens: 4 months before
- Applications processed and roles assigned: by 3 months before / 2 months before respectively
- Training session: 1 month before (mandatory — see [volunteers/volunteer-program-guide.md](../volunteers/volunteer-program-guide))

Full timeline detail: [planning/master-planning-timeline.md](../planning/master-planning-timeline).

## Process

1. Distribute [templates/volunteer-application-form.md](../templates/volunteer-application-form) through social channels, community partners, and student ambassadors ([partnerships-and-outreach/school-and-university-outreach.md](../partnerships-and-outreach/school-and-university-outreach)).
2. Match applicants to roles based on stated preference and availability (see role list in [volunteers/volunteer-program-guide.md](../volunteers/volunteer-program-guide)).
3. Confirm status on the form: Confirmed / Pending / Waitlist, and record assigned role, shift, and team lead.
4. Add confirmed volunteers to the Volunteers Group communication channel ([event-day/operations-runbook.md](../event-day/operations-runbook)).
5. Require attendance at the mandatory training session before event day.

## What the form captures

- Personal information and emergency contact
- Prior volunteering experience
- Preferred role (Registration Desk, Stage Support, Speaker Support, Media, Crowd Control, Technical Support, Setup/Teardown, or flexible)
- Availability by day/shift (Setup Day, Morning, Afternoon, Full Day, Teardown)
- T-shirt size
- Dietary restrictions (volunteers receive meals)
- Signed commitment agreement

## Success looks like

Tracked in the Volunteers database: confirmed vs. target, coverage by role, training completion rate, shift coverage. See [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup).

## If coverage is short

If any role or shift is under-covered close to the event, escalate to the Lead Organizer per [organisation/accountability-and-escalation.md](../organisation/accountability-and-escalation) — do not silently ask organizers to double up on event day without planning for it, since that directly conflicts with the volunteer-driven, anti-burnout principle.
