# Partnerships & Outreach — Strategy Overview

**Purpose:** The shared strategy across all four outreach segments (schools, training hubs, corporate/co-working, communities). Read this first, then go to your segment's playbook for templates and tracking.

**Owner:** Partnerships & Outreach Lead, with one named segment owner per track.
**Segment playbooks:** [school-and-university-outreach.md](school-and-university-outreach) · [training-hub-outreach.md](training-hub-outreach) · [corporate-and-coworking-outreach.md](corporate-and-coworking-outreach) · [community-organisation-outreach.md](community-organisation-outreach)

## Target audience segments

| Segment | Target audience | Key message | Expected reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Universities & Polytechnics | CS/IT students, tech clubs | Career development, networking | 500–1,000 students |
| Training Hubs & Bootcamps | Students, career switchers | Skill building, job opportunities | 200–400 learners |
| Corporate Organizations | Developers, digital teams | Professional development | 100–200 professionals |
| Co-working Spaces | Freelancers, startups | Community, business growth | 150–300 members |
| Tech Communities | Developers, enthusiasts | Knowledge sharing, networking | 300–500 members |
| Secondary Schools | STEM students, coders | Early exposure to tech | 200–400 students |

## Campaign objectives

- Drive ticket sales — target 150–400 attendees
- Build awareness — reach 10,000+ people in the Port Harcourt tech ecosystem
- Recruit volunteers — 30–50 event-day volunteers
- Identify speakers — discover new voices from the community
- Foster partnerships — 10+ community partnerships
- Build long-term community — grow monthly meetup attendance beyond this single event

## Key messaging pillars

**For students:** Learn from industry professionals · Build your professional network · Discover job/internship opportunities · Free student tickets available.

**For professionals:** Stay current with WordPress trends · Connect with potential clients/employers · Share your expertise · Give back to the community.

**For organizations:** Support the local tech ecosystem · Build brand awareness · Connect with talent · Demonstrate corporate social responsibility.

## Channels and cadence

| Channel | Best for | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Email outreach | Direct contact, detailed information | Weekly waves |
| Social media | Awareness, engagement | Daily posts |
| In-person visits | Schools, hubs, high-value targets | Bi-weekly |
| Phone calls | Follow-ups, urgent communication | As needed |
| WhatsApp groups | Community engagement, quick updates | 2–3x weekly |
| Flyers/posters | Campus, co-working spaces | One-time distribution |

## Campaign phases (6 months out to event)

### Phase 1: Foundation (6 months before)
- Weeks 1–2: finalize target lists, create campaign assets, set up tracking, assign segment owners, establish weekly check-ins
- Weeks 3–4: send first wave of emails to all segments, begin social campaign, schedule campus visits, contact top 10 priority organizations

### Phase 2: Active Engagement (5–4 months before)
- Weeks 5–8: follow up on all initial emails, conduct 2–3 campus visits/week, host first pre-event workshop, recruit student ambassadors, secure first 5 partnerships
- Weeks 9–12: launch ambassador program, distribute flyers at all partner locations, host 2–3 more workshops, begin corporate group sales, secure 10+ partnerships

### Phase 3: Conversion (3–2 months before)
- Weeks 13–16: announce early bird deadline, ramp up social frequency, send reminder emails, activate ambassador networks — target 50% of ticket goal
- Weeks 17–20: announce speaker lineup, share past-attendee testimonials, send final call emails, offer last-minute group discounts — target 80% of ticket goal

### Phase 4: Final Push (1 month before)
- Daily social media posts, personal outreach to key contacts, activate all partner networks, send event details to registered attendees — target 100% of ticket goal

## Success factors (what actually works)

- **Consistency** — regular outreach beats sporadic bursts
- **Personalization** — tailor messages to each segment, don't mail-merge a single template across all four
- **Follow-up** — most responses come after 2–3 touches, not the first email
- **Relationship-building** — think long-term partnership, not a one-time ask
- **Data-driven** — track everything on the dashboard and adjust based on what's converting
- **Team collaboration** — share wins, challenges, and learnings across segments in the weekly report
