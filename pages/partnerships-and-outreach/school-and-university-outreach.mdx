# School & University Outreach

**Purpose:** Playbook for the student segment: universities, polytechnics, and STEM-focused secondary schools. Strategy context: [partnerships-and-outreach/outreach-strategy-overview.md](outreach-strategy-overview).

**Owner:** Schools segment owner within Partnerships & Outreach.

## Target institutions

**Universities (primary targets):** University of Port Harcourt (UNIPORT), Rivers State University (RSU), University of Science & Technology (UST), Federal University Otuoke (FUO).

**Polytechnics & colleges:** Captain Elechi Amadi Polytechnic, Port Harcourt Polytechnic, Ken Saro-Wiwa Polytechnic.

**Secondary schools (STEM focus):** schools with active coding/computer clubs, schools participating in tech competitions, private schools with strong ICT programs.

## Key contacts to identify

| Role | Where to find | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| HOD Computer Science | University department office | Endorsement, student access |
| Student Affairs Officer | Student affairs division | Event approval, promotion |
| Tech Club President | Campus tech groups | Peer promotion, organization |
| Student Union Leaders | Student government | Wide reach, credibility |
| ICT Director | Secondary schools | Permission, logistics |
| Computer Teachers | Secondary schools | Student engagement |

## Email template: HODs/Professors

**Subject:** WordCamp Port Harcourt: Opportunity for Your Students

> Dear Professor [Name],
>
> I hope this email finds you well. My name is [Your Name], and I'm organizing WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year], a community conference for WordPress developers and digital professionals.
>
> We're reaching out to Computer Science departments across Port Harcourt because we believe your students would greatly benefit from attending. WordCamp offers:
> - Practical workshops on web development
> - Networking with industry professionals
> - Career guidance and job opportunities
> - FREE student tickets (limited availability)
>
> Would you be willing to:
> 1. Share information about WordCamp with your students?
> 2. Allow us to present briefly at a department gathering?
> 3. Post our flyer on your department notice board?
>
> We can provide presentation materials, flyers, and any information you need. We're also happy to offer certificates of attendance for students who participate.
>
> Thank you for considering this opportunity to support your students' professional development.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]
> WordCamp Port Harcourt Organizing Team
> [Email] | [Phone]

## Student Ambassador Program

Recruit 2–3 ambassadors per university.

- **Benefits:** free ticket, exclusive swag, certificate, networking
- **Responsibilities:** share on social media, distribute flyers, encourage classmates

Ambassador sign-up captures: name, school, course/year, social media handles, and commitment level (High/Medium/Low). Track ambassadors on the dashboard alongside the tracking sheet in [templates/outreach-tracking-sheets.md](../templates/outreach-tracking-sheets).
