# Corporate & Co-working Space Outreach

**Purpose:** Playbook for corporate organizations, tech companies, and co-working spaces — engaging professionals and building a sponsorship-adjacent pipeline. Strategy context: [partnerships-and-outreach/outreach-strategy-overview.md](outreach-strategy-overview).

**Owner:** Corporate segment owner within Partnerships & Outreach. Coordinates closely with [sponsorship/](../sponsorship/) since this track often surfaces sponsor leads.

## Target organizations

**Tech companies:** software development companies, digital agencies, web hosting providers, e-commerce platforms, fintech companies.

**Corporate organizations:** banks with digital teams, telecommunications companies, oil & gas companies with IT departments, government agencies.

**Co-working spaces:** Ventures Platform, Hub One, Stonebridge, local co-working spaces, business incubators.

## Dual-track approach

- **Track 1 — Attendee Recruitment:** encourage employees to attend individually or in groups.
- **Track 2 — Sponsorship:** position the same relationship as a sponsorship opportunity. If a corporate contact shows sponsorship interest, hand off to [sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook.md](../sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook) rather than running two separate conversations with the same company.

## Co-working space partnership

**What WordCamp offers:** free tickets for co-working space members, recognition as Community Partner, cross-promotion opportunities, potential workshop collaboration.

**What co-working spaces offer:** flyer/postcard distribution, email newsletter inclusion, social media promotion, event space for a pre-WordCamp meetup.
