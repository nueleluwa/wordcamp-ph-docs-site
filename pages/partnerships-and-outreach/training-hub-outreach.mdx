# Training Hub Outreach

**Purpose:** Playbook for coding bootcamps, digital skills centers, and professional development institutions. Strategy context: [partnerships-and-outreach/outreach-strategy-overview.md](outreach-strategy-overview).

**Owner:** Training Hubs segment owner within Partnerships & Outreach.

## Target training hubs

**Coding bootcamps:** Andela Learning Community (ALC), Google Developer Groups (GDG) Port Harcourt, local web development bootcamps, Python/Django Nigeria community.

**Digital skills centers:** NIIT Port Harcourt, New Horizons Nigeria, Aptech Computer Education, local ICT training centers.

**Professional development:** project management training centers, digital marketing academies, UI/UX design schools, freelancer training programs.

## Partnership value proposition

**What WordCamp offers training hubs:**
- Free tickets for their students/graduates
- Speaking opportunities for their instructors (subject to normal program review; see [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance))
- Booth space to showcase their programs
- Cross-promotion on social media
- Certificate of partnership

**What training hubs offer WordCamp:**
- Promotion to their student base
- Venue for pre-event workshops
- Speaker referrals
- Volunteer recruitment
- Credibility and reach

## Partnership tiers

| Tier | Hub commitment | WordCamp benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Platinum Partner | Promote to 200+ students, host workshop | 10 free tickets, speaking slot, booth, logo on website |
| Gold Partner | Promote to 100+ students | 5 free tickets, booth space, logo on website |
| Silver Partner | Promote to 50+ students | 3 free tickets, logo on website |
| Community Partner | Share with students | 2 free tickets, mention in newsletter |

## Pre-event workshop collaboration

Offer to co-host workshops at training hubs before WordCamp: 2–3 hours, hands-on, free for students.

**Topics:** Introduction to WordPress · Building Your First Website · WordPress for Freelancers · Getting Started with WooCommerce.

**Benefits:** builds awareness, provides value to the hub's students, recruits attendees.
