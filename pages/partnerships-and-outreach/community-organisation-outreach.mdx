# Community Organization Outreach

**Purpose:** Playbook for tech communities, professional associations, and affinity groups — the segment most likely to become long-term, recurring partners rather than one-time contacts. Strategy context: [partnerships-and-outreach/outreach-strategy-overview.md](outreach-strategy-overview).

**Owner:** Communities segment owner within Partnerships & Outreach.

## Target communities

**Tech communities:** Google Developer Groups (GDG), Facebook Developer Circles, Laravel Nigeria, Python Nigeria, React/Angular/Vue communities, DevOps Nigeria.

**Professional associations:** Nigerian Computer Society (NCS), Computer Professionals Registration Council (CPN), Nigerian Association of Computer Science Students (NACOSS), PMI Nigeria Chapter.

**Women in Tech groups:** Women Techmakers, She Code Africa, Ladies in Tech, Women in Data.

## Partnership levels

| Level | Partner provides | WordCamp provides |
|---|---|---|
| Champion | Promote to 500+ members, speaker referral | 10 tickets, logo, booth, shoutout |
| Advocate | Promote to 200+ members | 5 tickets, logo, mention |
| Supporter | Promote to 100+ members | 3 tickets, logo |
| Friend | Share with members | 2 tickets, mention |

## Email template: Community Partnership Outreach

**Subject:** Let's Partner: WordCamp Port Harcourt + [Community Name]

> Hello [Community Leader],
>
> I hope you're well! I'm [Your Name] from WordCamp Port Harcourt, and I've been following the amazing work [Community Name] is doing in the Port Harcourt tech ecosystem.
>
> We're organizing WordCamp Port Harcourt [Year] on [Date], and we'd love to partner with [Community Name] to make it a success for everyone.
>
> **WHY PARTNER WITH WORDCAMP?**
> - Give your members access to WordPress learning opportunities
> - Cross-promote your community to our audience
> - Build relationships with other tech communities
> - Strengthen the overall Port Harcourt tech ecosystem
>
> **PARTNERSHIP OPTIONS:**
> We have flexible partnership levels based on what works for your community:
> - Share WordCamp with your members (newsletter, social, meetups)
> - Refer potential speakers from your network
> - Co-host a pre-WordCamp meetup
> - Volunteer together at the event
>
> In return, we offer tickets, recognition, and cross-promotion.
>
> Would you be interested in a quick chat to explore how we can collaborate?
>
> Cheers,
> [Your Name]
> Community Partnerships, WordCamp Port Harcourt
> [Email] | [Phone]

## Collaboration ideas beyond a single email

- **Joint Meetups** — co-host pre-WordCamp events
- **Speaker Exchange** — invite community leaders to speak
- **Cross-Promotion** — share each other's events
- **Volunteer Pool** — recruit volunteers from partner communities
- **Content Collaboration** — co-create blog posts or videos
- **Discount Codes** — special registration codes for community members
