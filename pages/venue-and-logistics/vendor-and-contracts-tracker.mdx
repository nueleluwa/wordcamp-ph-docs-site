# Vendor & Contracts Tracker

**Purpose:** Every external vendor commitment WCPH depends on, tracked as a signed contract — not a verbal agreement or a WhatsApp message.

**Owner:** Logistics Lead

## Contract categories required before the event

☐ Venue rental agreement signed
☐ Catering contract confirmed
☐ AV equipment rental agreement
☐ Security service contract
☐ Printing services contract
☐ Transportation contracts (if applicable)
☐ All contracts reviewed and signed

## What to track per vendor

Maintain on the Logistics dashboard (see [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup)), not in this document:

- Vendor name and category
- Contract status (Not Started / Draft / Signed)
- Payment status and terms
- Delivery/setup date
- Backup vendor contact, where one exists (see [venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist.md](nigerian-logistics-checklist) — several categories explicitly call for a backup: catering, AV equipment, internet)

Dashboard views to maintain: All Vendors (full list), By Category, By Status, Payment Tracking (filtered by payment status).

## KPIs

Vendors Confirmed / Total; Budget Spent vs. Allocated; Pending Contracts; Upcoming Deliveries. See [reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework.md](../reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework) and [planning/budget-and-finance-principles.md](../planning/budget-and-finance-principles).

## Payment

Vendor payments should follow the same bank-transfer-first, transparently-recorded approach used for sponsor invoicing — see [planning/budget-and-finance-principles.md](../planning/budget-and-finance-principles). Final vendor payments are processed as part of post-event financial closeout — see [post-event/post-event-workflow.md](../post-event/post-event-workflow).
