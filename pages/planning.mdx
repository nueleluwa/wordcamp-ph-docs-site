# Planning

The event-wide timeline, weekly rhythm, and budget principles everything else nests inside.

### [Master Planning Timeline](planning/master-planning-timeline)
The single, org-wide timeline from team formation to event day. Every team's detailed playbook nests inside this timeline — if a team's plan and this timeline ever disagree on timing, this document wins until updated.

### [Weekly Operating Rhythm](planning/weekly-operating-rhythm)
The recurring cadence that keeps every team aligned without requiring constant ad-hoc coordination. This is what makes the accountability system in accountability-and-escalation actually run week to week.

### [Budget & Finance Principles](planning/budget-and-finance-principles)
How money moves through WCPHC — revenue targets, expense categories, payment methods, and the transparency obligation WordCamp Central requires. This document consolidates financial information that was previously scattered across sponsorship, logistics, and post-event material.
