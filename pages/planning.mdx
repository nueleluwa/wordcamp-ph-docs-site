# Planning

The event-wide timeline, weekly rhythm, and budget principles everything else nests inside.

### [Master Planning Timeline](planning/master-planning-timeline)
The single, org-wide timeline from team formation to event day. Every team's playbook nests inside this timeline. If a team's plan and this timeline ever disagree on timing, this document wins until it's updated.

### [Weekly Operating Rhythm](planning/weekly-operating-rhythm)
The recurring cadence that keeps every team aligned without constant back-and-forth. It's what makes the accountability system in accountability-and-escalation actually run, week to week.

### [Budget & Finance Principles](planning/budget-and-finance-principles)
How money moves through WCPHC: revenue targets, expense categories, payment methods, and the transparency WordCamp Central requires. This brings together financial information that used to be scattered across sponsorship, logistics, and post-event material.
