# Partnerships & Outreach

Strategy and segment playbooks for schools, training hubs, corporates, and communities.

### [Outreach Strategy Overview](partnerships-and-outreach/outreach-strategy-overview)
The shared strategy across all four segments: schools, training hubs, corporate and co-working, and communities. Read this first, then go to your segment's playbook for templates and tracking.

### [School & University Outreach](partnerships-and-outreach/school-and-university-outreach)
The playbook for the student segment: universities, polytechnics, and STEM-focused secondary schools. Strategy context: outreach-strategy-overview.

### [Training Hub Outreach](partnerships-and-outreach/training-hub-outreach)
The playbook for coding bootcamps, digital skills centers, and professional development institutions. Strategy context: outreach-strategy-overview.

### [Corporate & Co-working Space Outreach](partnerships-and-outreach/corporate-and-coworking-outreach)
The playbook for corporate organizations, tech companies, and co-working spaces: engaging professionals and building a pipeline that sits alongside sponsorship. Strategy context: outreach-strategy-overview.

### [Community Organization Outreach](partnerships-and-outreach/community-organisation-outreach)
The playbook for tech communities, professional associations, and affinity groups. This segment is the most likely to become a long-term, recurring partner rather than a one-time contact. Strategy context: outreach-strategy-overview.
