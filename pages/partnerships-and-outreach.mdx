# Partnerships & Outreach

Strategy and segment playbooks for schools, training hubs, corporates, and communities.

### [Partnerships & Outreach — Strategy Overview](partnerships-and-outreach/outreach-strategy-overview)
The shared strategy across all four outreach segments (schools, training hubs, corporate/co-working, communities). Read this first, then go to your segment's playbook for templates and tracking.

### [School & University Outreach](partnerships-and-outreach/school-and-university-outreach)
Playbook for the student segment — universities, polytechnics, and STEM-focused secondary schools. Strategy context: outreach-strategy-overview.

### [Training Hub Outreach](partnerships-and-outreach/training-hub-outreach)
Playbook for coding bootcamps, digital skills centers, and professional development institutions. Strategy context: outreach-strategy-overview.

### [Corporate & Co-working Space Outreach](partnerships-and-outreach/corporate-and-coworking-outreach)
Playbook for corporate organizations, tech companies, and co-working spaces — engaging professionals and building a sponsorship-adjacent pipeline. Strategy context: outreach-strategy-overview.

### [Community Organization Outreach](partnerships-and-outreach/community-organisation-outreach)
Playbook for tech communities, professional associations, and affinity groups — the segment most likely to become long-term, recurring partners rather than one-time contacts. Strategy context: outreach-strategy-overview.
