# Sponsorship

Tiers, outreach templates, and the full sponsor lifecycle from cold contact to thank-you.

### [Sponsorship Strategy & Tiers](sponsorship/sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers)
The reference tier structure and pipeline model for sponsorship. This is the lookup document. For the outreach process, see sponsorship-outreach-playbook. For what happens after a sponsor says yes, see sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.

### [Sponsorship Outreach Playbook](sponsorship/sponsorship-outreach-playbook)
The step-by-step process and email templates for moving a sponsor prospect from cold contact to confirmed. Tier reference: sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers. Post-confirmation steps: sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.

### [Sponsor Lifecycle & Fulfillment](sponsorship/sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment)
Everything that happens after a sponsor confirms: through the event, to the post-event thank-you. Getting a "yes" is the start of the relationship, not the end of the task.
