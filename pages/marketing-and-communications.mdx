# Marketing & Communications

Social media, long-form content, and direct attendee emails.

### [Social Media Playbook](marketing-and-communications/social-media-playbook)
Templates and posting cadence across the event lifecycle. For long-form content like articles and blog posts, see the content and articles guide. For direct attendee emails, see attendee communications.

### [Content & Articles Guide](marketing-and-communications/content-and-articles-guide)
Frameworks for longer content, like blog posts and articles, that builds community engagement all year round, not just around the event. For social posts, see the social media playbook.

### [Attendee Communications](marketing-and-communications/attendee-communications)
The direct emails sent to individual attendees, as opposed to broadcast social and content marketing. Owned by the Marketing & Communications Lead, working with Logistics on venue and parking details.
