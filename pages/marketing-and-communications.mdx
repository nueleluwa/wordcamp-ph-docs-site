# Marketing & Communications

Social media, long-form content, and direct attendee emails.

### [Social Media Playbook](marketing-and-communications/social-media-playbook)
Templates and cadence for social media across the event lifecycle. For long-form content (articles, blog posts), see content-and-articles-guide. For direct attendee emails, see attendee-communications.

### [Content & Articles Guide](marketing-and-communications/content-and-articles-guide)
Frameworks for longer-form content (blog posts, articles) that builds community engagement year-round, not just around the event. For social posts, see social-media-playbook.

### [Attendee Communications](marketing-and-communications/attendee-communications)
The direct, transactional emails sent to individual attendees, as distinct from broadcast social/content marketing. Owner: Marketing & Communications Lead, in coordination with Logistics for venue/parking details.
