# Organisation

Team structure, accountability, and the questions the team hasn't resolved yet.

### [Team Structure & Roles](organisation/team-structure-and-roles)
One page to answer "which team am I on, what does that team own, and who else is on it."

### [Accountability & Escalation](organisation/accountability-and-escalation)
WCPHC's accountability culture, made concrete. This is the page every other document points to when it asks "what happens if I can't complete a task?"

### [Open Decisions & Gaps](organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps)
A living list of things the team hasn't formally decided yet, so silence on a topic is never mistaken for an answer. Per accountability-and-escalation, an undecided item still needs an owner and a deadline. The owner here is "decide this," not "execute this."
