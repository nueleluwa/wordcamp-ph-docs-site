# Budget & Finance Principles

**Purpose:** How money moves through WCPH — revenue targets, expense categories, payment methods, and the transparency obligation WordCamp Central requires. This document consolidates financial information that was previously scattered across sponsorship, logistics, and post-event material.

**Owner:** Lead Organizer, with Logistics Lead handling vendor payments and Sponsorship Lead handling sponsor invoicing.
**Related policy:** [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance) — financial transparency is a hard requirement, not a best practice.

> **Open decision:** a detailed budget template, spending-approval thresholds, and an approver for expenses above that threshold are not yet formalized. See [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](../organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps).

## The financial target

**Break-even or better.** WCPH is run as a not-for-profit event under WordCamp Central rules — the goal is not to maximize surplus but to cover costs reliably while keeping tickets affordable and student access free. Net Profit/Loss (Revenue − Expenses) is tracked as an Event-Level KPI — see [reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework.md](../reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework).

## Expense categories to budget for

Derived from the logistics and vendor requirements across the event:

- Venue rental
- Power backup (generator rental, fuel)
- Internet (primary + backup connectivity)
- Catering (all sessions, dietary accommodations)
- AV equipment rental
- Security personnel
- Printing (badges, signage, banners, materials)
- Swag and merchandise
- Transportation/shuttle (if venue requires it)
- Speaker travel (where applicable)
- Volunteer t-shirts and meals

See [venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist.md](../venue-and-logistics/nigerian-logistics-checklist) for the full operational checklist behind these line items.

## Payment methods

**Bank transfer is the default payment method for sponsors and major vendors** — this is a deliberate Nigerian-context decision, not a fallback. Sponsor invoices should always include:

- Account Name
- Bank Name
- Account Number

See [sponsorship/sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment.md](../sponsorship/sponsor-lifecycle-and-fulfillment) for the invoicing step in the sponsor confirmation flow.

## Financial transparency requirement

WordCamp Central requires financial transparency. In practice this means:

- A budget vs. actual comparison is maintained throughout planning, not reconstructed after the event.
- The post-event financial closeout (reconciling expenses, processing final vendor payments, preparing a financial report) is mandatory — see [post-event/post-event-workflow.md](../post-event/post-event-workflow) "Within 1 Month."
- The financial summary is a required section of the post-event report — see [post-event/event-report-guide.md](../post-event/event-report-guide).
- Financial records are archived for future organizing teams, not discarded after reporting.

## Vendor contracts

All vendor commitments (venue, catering, AV, security, printing, transportation) should be documented as signed contracts before payment or the event, tracked in [venue-and-logistics/vendor-and-contracts-tracker.md](../venue-and-logistics/vendor-and-contracts-tracker).
