# Weekly Operating Rhythm

**Purpose:** The recurring cadence that keeps every team aligned without constant back-and-forth. This is what makes the accountability system in [organisation/accountability-and-escalation.md](../organisation/accountability-and-escalation) actually run week to week.

## The weekly cycle

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Weekly team check-in; each team plans its week | Each Team Lead |
| Tuesday | Outreach/email sends go out (sponsorship, partnerships) | Sponsorship Lead, Partnerships & Outreach Lead |
| Wednesday | Social content goes out; follow-up calls made | Marketing Lead, all teams |
| Thursday | Campus visits, in-person meetings, vendor check-ins | Partnerships & Outreach Lead, Logistics Lead |
| Friday | Update dashboards; submit weekly report | Every team member |

This rhythm applies from the moment outreach/sponsorship work begins (~7 months out) through the final week before the event.

## Weekly team meeting

Every team runs its own short weekly sync using the standard agenda in [templates/meeting-agenda-template.md](../templates/meeting-agenda-template):

1. Wins (5 min)
2. Metrics review against dashboard (10 min)
3. Blockers & support needed (15 min)
4. This week's priorities, with named owners (15 min)
5. Action items captured with owner + due date

## Weekly organizer meeting (all Team Leads + Lead Organizer)

A separate, shorter cross-team meeting where Team Leads surface:
- Cross-team dependencies that are stuck
- KPIs that are off track team-wide
- Items logged as "needing escalation" in weekly reports

This is where [organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps.md](../organisation/open-decisions-and-gaps) gets reviewed and updated.

## Weekly report

Every team submits one weekly report using [templates/weekly-report-template.md](../templates/weekly-report-template), due Friday. Reports aren't busywork. They're the primary mechanism that lets the Lead Organizer see the whole event's health without sitting in every team meeting. See [reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide.md](../reporting-and-kpis/weekly-team-report-guide) for how reports are used.

## Why this cadence, specifically

- **Tuesday sends / Friday reporting** gives a full week to measure response before the next report, so "Response Rate" numbers in weekly reports are meaningful rather than noise.
- **Monday planning before Tuesday execution** means teams walk into outreach day with that week's priorities already agreed, not decided on the fly.
- A fixed weekly report deadline (Friday) is what makes the dashboard's "Overdue" and "Due This Week" views (see [reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup.md](../reporting-and-kpis/dashboard-and-tracking-setup)) trustworthy. If reporting is sporadic, the dashboard silently goes stale.
