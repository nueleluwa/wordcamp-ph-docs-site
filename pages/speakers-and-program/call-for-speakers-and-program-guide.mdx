# Call for Speakers & Program Guide

**Purpose:** How WCPH sources, reviews, and schedules speakers, and how the program takes shape.
**Owner:** Program & Speakers Lead

## Timeline

| When | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 6 months before | Open Call for Speakers (CFP) |
| 5 months before | Review speaker applications; begin selection |
| 4 months before | Confirm first batch of speakers; announce keynote(s) publicly |
| 3 months before | Confirm all speakers; publish full schedule; send speaker information packets |
| 2 months before | Send speaker reminders; collect final materials (slides, bio, headshot) |
| 1 month before | Confirm speaker travel arrangements |

Full context: [planning/master-planning-timeline.md](../planning/master-planning-timeline).

## Review process

1. Applications collected through the CFP form (linked from the WordCamp website).
2. Each submission reviewed against session quality, relevance, and speaker diversity — reviewers record their assessment on the dashboard's "My Reviews" view.
3. Selections finalized by the Program Lead with input from reviewing organizers.
4. Selected speakers notified and moved into the confirmation workflow (info packet → schedule slot → reminders → final materials → travel arrangements).

## The non-negotiable rule

**Speaker slots cannot be purchased or influenced by sponsorship**, including the Platinum sponsor "keynote slot" benefit referenced in [sponsorship/sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers.md](../sponsorship/sponsorship-strategy-and-tiers) — any sponsor-affiliated speaker goes through the same review as every other submission. See [policies/wordcamp-central-compliance.md](../policies/wordcamp-central-compliance).

Full KPI reference: [reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework.md](../reporting-and-kpis/kpi-framework). Dashboard views to maintain: All Submissions (full list), By Status, By Category (talk category), My Reviews (filtered by reviewer), Sessions by Track.

## Event day

Speaker-facing roles on event day are covered in [event-day/operations-runbook.md](../event-day/operations-runbook):
- **Stage Control** — introduces speakers, manages timing, coordinates Q&A, signals time remaining
- **Speaker Prep Room** — welcomes arriving speakers, assists with AV setup, escorts to stage, collects feedback afterward

If a speaker doesn't show up, follow the response in [event-day/issue-response-playbook.md](../event-day/issue-response-playbook) (find a backup speaker or extend Q&A — Program Lead owns the call).

## Post-event

Speaker presentation slides and session recordings feed the recap content pipeline — see [post-event/post-event-workflow.md](../post-event/post-event-workflow) "Recap Content," and speaker feedback surveys are part of the post-event feedback collection.
